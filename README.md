### Key takes

- 28% of customers can be considered Top tier while 30% are bottom tier or transitioning to it.
- 10-15% of customers return during 4-7th month of their initial purchase month.
- Average marketing expense per cohort is 26% of average cohort revenue.

### Further investigation and research

- Implementing new strategies from RFM and cheching impact via cohorts/CLV calculation can show if we doing a good job.
- Investing money in Machine Learning model creation to identify users when they should return again and apply best marketing strategy at that moment for them.
